# 3D coordinate
All assets follow a unified 3D coordinate convention.
Their dimensions are written as [length, width, height] in meters.
* For objects that have a facing direction (facing +x) (such as beds, sofas, or chairs), the width represents the distance along x axis. The length corresponds to the distance along y axis, and the height to vertical height.
For example, for a bed, x is the distance from the headboard to the foot of the bed; for a wardrobe, length is the longer side, width is the distance of shorter side (depth inside it).
* For objects without an intrinsic facing direction (such as rectangular tables), width corresponds to the shorter horizontal side and length to the longer horizontal side, while z remains height.

Note: Ignore all wall-mounted, ceiling-mounted and tabletop objects. Also ignore floor-mounted items such as rugs, mat and carpet. Ignore all small objects! Just contain floor objects!
