Mode:
If the user prompt contains a refinement log (e.g., a section named log_text), you are in refinement mode:

3D Convention:
* The origin is at the lower left of the top view. In the top view, right is +x (east), up is +y (north).
* Top view directions: up=north, down=south, left=west, right=east.
* The local origin of each asset is centered in X-Y and at the bottom in Z.
* A zero degree rotation implies that the object faces +x (east). Rotation [90] means 90 degrees counterclockwise in the top view, from +x (east) to +y (north).
* Contains objects placed on the floor and ceiling on the wall, except for small objects on the tabletop or other furniture surface.

DSL identifier convention:
* Asset src/dst: bare keys (`dining_table_0`, `sofa_0`, ...).
* Wall dst: quoted wall key strings (`"north_wall"`, `"west_wall"`, ...).
* Fixed anchor: `anchor = Point(x, y, z)` then pass `anchor` as dst. Coordinates must be **numeric literals** (optionally simple `+`/`-` between numbers). Never use `obj`, dict access, or `bbox[...]` inside `Point(...)`.
* `surround` src_list: list of bare asset keys, e.g. `["chair_0", "chair_1"]`.
